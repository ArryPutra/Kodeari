
# kodeari Design Direction

## Product
kodeari provides website development services for businesses
of different sizes and industries in Indonesia.

## Visual Identity
- Modern, minimal, professional, and approachable.
- White is the dominant background color.
- Use cobalt blue as a restrained accent.
- Use deep navy for primary text.
- Typography, spacing, and composition should create
  the visual identity, not decorative effects.

## Art Direction
- Editorial composition with deliberate asymmetry where useful.
- Strong typography and intentional whitespace.
- Use realistic website previews when demonstrating
  the quality of website development.
- Use subtle geometric shapes or background textures
  only when they improve composition.
- Prefer original, purposeful visual decisions over
  common landing page patterns.

## Hero
- Centered marketing content in the upper area.
- Website preview positioned below the marketing content.
- Natural scrolling between the headline and preview.
- Background should remain predominantly white.
- Any blue gradient or geometric pattern must be subtle
  and must not compete with the headline.
- Keep the composition visually interesting without
  relying on floating decorative elements.

## Copywriting
- Write natural Indonesian for business owners.
- Be clear, concise, and specific.
- Avoid generic AI marketing language.
- Do not invent testimonials, client logos, statistics,
  awards, pricing, or performance claims.

## Motion
- Use restrained transitions with a clear purpose.
- Avoid perpetual floating animations and excessive parallax.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Responsive Design
- Preserve the visual hierarchy across desktop and mobile.
- Avoid horizontal overflow and awkward text wrapping.
- Do not reduce desktop layouts into cramped mobile layouts.

## Design Review
Before adding any visual effect or component, explain
what purpose it serves.
Remove elements that do not improve communication,
usability, brand identity, or visual composition.
